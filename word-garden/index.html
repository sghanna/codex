A few simple things

Let’s make a word.

CAT

Use the letters in the circle to find words in the crossword. Filled letters help you discover the next word.

  1. Choose letters in order. Use each letter tile once.
  2. Review your word. Undo removes the last letter. × clears it.
  3. Press Submit. Words have at least three letters.

Stuck? Shuffle for a fresh view, or use a free Hint to reveal a letter. There’s no limit on guesses or hints.

Found another word? Accepted words outside the grid count as bonuses. Everyday plurals and word forms are welcome. Names and abbreviations don’t count; the bonus list may not contain every dictionary word.

Choose Swipe letters in settings to draw a word. Trace back to the previous letter to undo, then release and press Submit.

Keyboard: type letters, Backspace to undo, Escape to clear, Enter to submit. Tab moves between controls.
