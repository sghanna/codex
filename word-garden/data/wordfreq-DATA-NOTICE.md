# wordfreq data attribution

The Word Garden bonus lexicon was filtered using English frequency estimates from **wordfreq 3.1.1**, created by Robyn Speer and contributors. The package is available at <https://pypi.org/project/wordfreq/3.1.1/>; its source and data licensing notes are at <https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq#license>.

wordfreq's code is Apache 2.0 licensed, while its frequency data may be redistributed under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0**. The Word Garden `bonus-lexicon.txt` and the bonus vocabulary arrays derived from it in `puzzles.js` are an adapted subset and are shared under that same data license. The [CC BY-SA 4.0 license text](CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt) is included locally. Changes from wordfreq data: no frequency numbers were copied; ESDB words were admitted when their wordfreq English Zipf estimate was at least 3.0, then selected for the available letter racks, with explicit exclusions and authored additions. The ESDB source has its own notice in [ESDB-Copyright.txt](ESDB-Copyright.txt).

As described in wordfreq's licensing notes, its English data draws on Google Books Ngrams, the Leeds Internet Corpus, Wikipedia, ParaCrawl, OpenSubtitles, and SUBTLEX-US. Credit for SUBTLEX-US belongs to Marc Brysbaert and collaborators; SUBTLEX remains freely available data. Credit for the OpenSubtitles contribution belongs to the OpenSubtitles project. See wordfreq's source documentation for its detailed citations and source terms. Individual Word Garden bonus entries cannot be traced to one source corpus because wordfreq combines the sources into one estimate.
